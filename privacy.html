27 Rounds

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

27 Rounds does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No servers. Your workouts, scores and settings are stored only on your device. They're never sent anywhere.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Sharing is up to you. When you tap Share, the app creates an image of your score and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice.
  • Deleting. Settings → Erase all data removes everything. Deleting the app does the same.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
