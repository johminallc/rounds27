27 Rounds

Support

27 Rounds counts, paces and scores the Cindy workout: 5 pull-ups, 10 push-ups and 15 squats, as many rounds as you can in 20 minutes.

Need help or found a bug?

Email johminallc@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I count? Tap anywhere on the screen after each movement. The screen color tells you what's next: orange for pull-ups, yellow for push-ups, green for squats. If you'd rather tap once per round, switch to "Tap each round" before you start and add your leftover reps at the end.

I tapped by mistake. Use the undo button at the top right of the workout screen. After the workout, "Fix score" lets you correct rounds and reps.

What's the pace ghost? It's who you're racing: your personal record replayed round by round, the viral 27-round pace, or a target you choose. The pill under the clock shows how far ahead or behind you are.

Why didn't my score count as a PR? Workouts ended early are saved but don't count toward PRs. PRs are kept separately for Rx, Scaled and Beginner.

I can't hear the voice coach. Check that it's switched on in Settings and that your ringer volume is up. Locking the phone mid-workout stops the voice cues; the clock and score stay correct.

Where is my data? Only on your phone. There's no account and no cloud backup, so deleting the app deletes your history.
